Your summary (150-200 words):

The Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 provides for the division of the former state into two Union Territories: Jammu and Kashmir with a legislative assembly, and Ladakh without one. The Act outlines the structure, powers, and functioning of the Legislative Assembly in Jammu and Kashmir, including provisions related to membership, disqualification, legislative powers, and procedures for passing laws. It defines the role of the Lieutenant Governor, including powers to address the Assembly, grant assent to bills, and issue ordinances. The Act also covers financial procedures such as budgeting, appropriation of funds, and grants. It includes provisions for delimitation of constituencies and representation based on census data. Additionally, it addresses administrative arrangements for Ladakh and abolishes the Legislative Council of the former state. Overall, the Act restructures governance and aligns it with the Union Territory administrative framework.
