The National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 presents a comprehensive framework for transforming India's education system to make it more inclusive, equitable, and holistic. It emphasizes the importance of early childhood care and education as the foundation for lifelong learning, along with achieving universal foundational literacy and numeracy. The policy aims to reduce dropout rates and ensure access to quality education at all levels. It proposes a shift from rote learning to a more engaging, multidisciplinary, and skill-oriented curriculum. In higher education, the policy envisions restructuring institutions into multidisciplinary universities and promoting research through initiatives such as the National Research Foundation. It also highlights the integration of vocational education and the use of technology in learning. Furthermore, NEP stresses teacher training, governance reforms, and increased public investment in education. Overall, it aims to build an education system that supports national development, social equity, and global competitiveness by 2040.
