The Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC) outlines the legal framework governing criminal law procedures in India, particularly focusing on powers of courts, police, and procedures for arrest and trial. It specifies the sentencing powers of High Courts, Sessions Courts, and Magistrates, and details how sentences should be applied in cases involving multiple offences. The document also elaborates on the powers of police officers, including arrest without warrant, procedures during arrest, and rights of arrested individuals such as the right to legal representation. It emphasizes safeguards such as informing the arrested person of grounds of arrest and ensuring that detention does not exceed 24 hours without judicial oversight. Additionally, it defines responsibilities of the public in assisting law enforcement and reporting certain offences. Overall, the CrPC ensures procedural fairness, accountability of authorities, and protection of individual rights within the criminal justice system.
