The Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 provides for the reorganisation of the former state into two Union Territories: Jammu and Kashmir, and Ladakh. It outlines the structure and functioning of the Legislative Assembly in Jammu and Kashmir, including provisions related to membership, disqualifications, legislative powers, and procedures for passing bills. The Act defines the role of the Lieutenant Governor, including powers to address the Assembly and promulgate ordinances. It also addresses financial governance, including budget procedures and appropriation of funds. The Act establishes provisions for delimitation of constituencies and representation based on census data. Additionally, it includes rules regarding official language, privileges of members, and administrative arrangements for Ladakh. Overall, the Act restructures governance, aligns it with Union Territory administration, and defines legislative and executive authority in the reorganized regions.
