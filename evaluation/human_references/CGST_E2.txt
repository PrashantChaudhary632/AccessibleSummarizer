The Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017 provides the legal framework for levying and collecting GST on intra-state supply of goods and services in India. It defines key terms such as taxable supply, non-taxable supply, and actionable claims, and establishes the scope and applicability of GST across the country. The Act outlines provisions for tax liability, registration of taxpayers, and procedures for filing returns and maintaining records. It also introduces mechanisms for input tax credit, allowing businesses to reduce tax burden by claiming credit for taxes already paid on inputs. The Act establishes authorities such as GST officers and the Goods and Services Tax Appellate Tribunal for dispute resolution. It includes provisions for audits, assessments, penalties, and offences to ensure compliance. Overall, the CGST Act aims to create a unified and efficient indirect tax system, reduce cascading taxes, and improve ease of doing business.
