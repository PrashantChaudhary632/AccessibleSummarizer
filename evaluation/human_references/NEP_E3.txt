The National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 is a comprehensive reform plan aimed at transforming India's education system to make it more inclusive, flexible, and skill-oriented. It focuses on improving both school and higher education. At the school level, it introduces a new 5+3+3+4 structure, emphasizes early childhood care, and prioritizes foundational literacy and numeracy. The policy promotes experiential learning, reduced rote memorization, and multidisciplinary approaches. In higher education, NEP aims to create a flexible system with multiple entry and exit options, integration of vocational education, and the establishment of a National Research Foundation to boost research. It also proposes restructuring institutions into multidisciplinary universities and colleges. The policy highlights the importance of teacher training, use of technology in education, and promotion of Indian languages and culture. It also focuses on equitable access to education for all sections of society. Overall, NEP 2020 aims to build a modern, globally competitive education system by 2040 that supports innovation, critical thinking, and holistic development.
