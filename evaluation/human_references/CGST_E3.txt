The Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017 governs the taxation of goods and services within a state in India. It is part of the GST framework, which replaced multiple indirect taxes to create a unified tax system. The Act defines how tax is levied, collected, and administered on intra-state supplies of goods and services. It includes key definitions such as taxable supply, non-taxable supply, and associated enterprises. The Act also outlines provisions for registration of taxpayers, filing of returns, payment of taxes, and input tax credit mechanisms that allow businesses to reduce tax burden. The Act establishes authorities like the GST Appellate Tribunal to handle disputes and appeals. It has been amended multiple times to improve compliance and address practical challenges. The document provided is an updated version for reference purposes and does not have legal force by itself. Overall, the CGST Act simplifies taxation, improves transparency, and aims to promote ease of doing business in India.
