The National Rural Health Mission (NRHM) is a government initiative aimed at improving healthcare delivery in rural India by strengthening public health infrastructure and ensuring accessible and affordable services. The framework outlines specific targets such as training Accredited Social Health Activists (ASHAs), establishing village health committees, and upgrading sub-centres, primary health centres, and community health centres. It sets phased timelines for implementation, monitoring mechanisms, and evaluation processes. The mission emphasizes decentralization through district-level planning and community participation to enhance accountability. It also focuses on improving maternal and child health, reducing mortality rates, and addressing gaps in rural healthcare systems. Financial support through grants and institutional strengthening is a key component. Overall, NRHM seeks to build a responsive, equitable, and efficient public health system that reaches every village and improves health outcomes across underserved populations.
