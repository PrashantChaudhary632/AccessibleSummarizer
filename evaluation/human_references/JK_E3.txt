The Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 reorganizes the former state of Jammu and Kashmir into two Union Territories: Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh. It defines the administrative, legislative, and governance structures for these new entities. The Act establishes a Legislative Assembly for Jammu and Kashmir with defined powers, while Ladakh is administered directly by a Lieutenant Governor without a legislature. It outlines rules related to law-making, financial procedures, and the relationship between the Union government and the Union Territories. The Act also includes provisions for delimitation of constituencies, representation, and applicability of laws. It specifies that laws made by Parliament will override conflicting laws made by the Legislative Assembly. Additionally, it covers administrative aspects such as the roles of the Lieutenant Governor, Council of Ministers, and legislative procedures. Overall, the Act aims to restructure governance for better administration, integration, and development of the region under the Indian Union.
