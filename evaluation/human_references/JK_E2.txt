The Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 reorganised the former state of Jammu and Kashmir into two Union Territories: Jammu and Kashmir (with a legislative assembly) and Ladakh (without a legislature). The Act lays down provisions for governance, including the role of the Lieutenant Governor, legislative powers, and functioning of the Legislative Assembly. It defines rules regarding membership, disqualification, voting procedures, and privileges of legislators. The Act also addresses legislative processes such as passing of bills, financial procedures, and the relationship between laws made by Parliament and the Union Territory legislature. It includes provisions for delimitation of constituencies, ensuring representation based on population data. Additionally, it covers administrative structures for Ladakh and provisions related to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. Overall, the Act establishes a new administrative and political framework for the governance of these regions under the Union.
