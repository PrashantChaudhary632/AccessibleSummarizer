The Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017 establishes the legal framework for the levy and collection of tax on intra-state supply of goods and services in India. It extends across the entire country and has been amended through multiple finance acts to address evolving economic needs. The Act defines key terms such as taxable supply, non-taxable supply, and associated enterprises, ensuring clarity in interpretation. It also outlines administrative mechanisms, including authorities like the Goods and Services Tax Appellate Tribunal, which handles disputes and appeals. The law integrates provisions from related tax systems such as Integrated GST and Union Territory GST, ensuring uniform taxation. While the document provided is a compiled version for reference and not legally binding, it reflects the structure and scope of GST implementation. Overall, the Act aims to streamline indirect taxation, improve compliance, and create a unified national market.
