The National Rural Health Mission (NRHM) is a government initiative aimed at improving healthcare access and quality in rural areas of India. It focuses on strengthening public health infrastructure and addressing gaps in service delivery, especially in underserved regions. The framework outlines plans to expand healthcare facilities such as Sub-Centres, Primary Health Centres (PHCs), Community Health Centres (CHCs), and district hospitals. It also introduces Accredited Social Health Activists (ASHAs) to act as a link between the community and the health system. The mission emphasizes community participation through Village Health and Sanitation Committees and aims to make healthcare more accountable and accessible. It also focuses on improving maternal and child health, reducing mortality rates, and controlling diseases. Additionally, NRHM includes timelines, funding mechanisms, and monitoring systems to track progress. Overall, it aims to provide affordable, equitable, and quality healthcare services to rural populations by strengthening both infrastructure and community involvement.
