The Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC) is a key law that defines the procedures for handling criminal cases in India. It outlines the powers of courts, police, and other authorities in maintaining law and order and ensuring justice. The document includes provisions related to sentencing powers of different courts such as High Courts, Sessions Courts, and Magistrates. It also details police powers, including arrest procedures, rights of arrested individuals, and duties of officers. For example, a person arrested must be informed of the reason for arrest and has the right to legal counsel. The law ensures safeguards such as limiting detention without a magistrate's approval to 24 hours. Additionally, CrPC explains procedures for summons, warrants, and trials, and defines the responsibilities of the public in assisting law enforcement. Overall, it provides a structured framework for investigation, arrest, trial, and punishment, ensuring that criminal justice is carried out fairly and systematically while protecting individual rights.
