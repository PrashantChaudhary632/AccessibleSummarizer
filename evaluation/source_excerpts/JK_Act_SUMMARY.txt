ACCESSIBLE SUMMARY: J&K Reorganisation Act, 2019
============================================================

KEY POINTS
------------------------------
  1. Governor of existing State to be common Lieutenant Governor.
  2. No person will be a member both of Parliament and of the Legislative Assembly.
  3. If a law made by the Legislative Assembly is repugnant to any provision of a law.
  4. Law made by Legislative Assembly of the Union territory will, to the extent of the repugnancy, be void.
  5. When a Bill has been passed by the Legislative Assembly, it will be presented to the Lieutenant Governor.
  6. The Legislative Assembly may make rules for regulating, subject to the provisions of this Act, its procedure.
  7. No discussion will take place in the Legislative Assembly with respect to the conduct of any judge of the Supreme Court or of a HighCourt.
