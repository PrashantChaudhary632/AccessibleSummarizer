CHAPTER I PRELIMINARY (Legislative Department) New Delhi, the 12th April, 2017/Chaitra 22, 1939 (Saka) The following Act of Parliament received the assent of the President on the 12th April, 2017, and is hereby published for general information:— THE CENTRAL GOODS AND SERVICES TAX ACT, 2017 NO. 12 OF 2017 [12th April, 2017.] An Act to make a provision for levy and collection of tax on intra-State supply of goods or services or both by the Central Government and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. BE it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-eighth Year of the Republic of India as follows:— 1. Short title, extent and commencement.— (1) This Act may be called the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017.
